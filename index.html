Invoice details

Order number
Shared with Supply and Customer Confirmation.
Invoice type
Invoice and Proforma Invoice are addressed to the customer at full selling price. Commission Invoice is addressed to the supplier, with unit prices calculated as the margin (selling price minus purchase price) on each product.
Final delivery
Saved separately from the originally ordered ETA — the actual delivery date, once known.
Final quantities
Final quantities are saved separately from the ordered quantity on the Order page, so the invoice can reflect the actual delivered amount (e.g. temperature-corrected vs ambient). Leave blank to invoice at the originally ordered quantity.
Purchase side adjustment
A positive or negative amount to balance the purchase side against the supplier's actual invoice total — for internal cost/profit tracking only, never shown on the customer Invoice, Proforma, or Commission Invoice.
